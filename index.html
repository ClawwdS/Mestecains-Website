NOT JUST A PLACE ON THE MAP

A mountain village.
A whole lot
of heart.

Set in Romania’s Bucovina region, Mestecanis is a place to slow down and take in the Carpathian foothills. Village lanes lead past gardens and wooden gates, while forest paths and open meadows begin just beyond home.

Discover the rhythms of village life, the landscapes of the Carpathians, and the traditions that make Bucovina distinctive. We’d love you to experience it at your own pace.

WE’LL PUT THE KETTLE ON

A little planning.
A lovely day out.

Bring sturdy shoes, a light for changing mountain weather, and time to wander village lanes and nearby trails.

Begin in the village

Take a gentle walk through the village first, then ask a local host about nearby paths and places to stop. Opening hours and services can vary with the season.

Explore on foot

Explore at an easy pace and allow extra time for steep or uneven ground. Trail conditions can change after rain, so check locally before setting out into the hills.

Make it a family day

Choose a shorter village walk, take breaks for the views, and check trail conditions before heading uphill. Check event details for age guidance and accessibility information.

Rain in the forecast?

Mountain weather can turn quickly. Ask your accommodation or local host about indoor options and current road conditions before travelling.
