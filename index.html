NOT JUST A PLACE ON THE MAP

A small town.
A whole lot
of heart.

Alderwick is the sort of place where a short stroll turns into a long conversation. Where independent shops still put their own names above the door, and the countryside begins just beyond the last garden gate.

From our old market square to the paths along the River Alder, every corner has a story. We’d love you to become part of it.

WE’LL PUT THE KETTLE ON

A little planning.
A lovely day out.

Bring comfortable shoes, an appetite, and a little time to get lost in the lanes.

Start at the market square

The square is the heart of the town and a lovely first stop. Pick up a coffee, browse the independent shops, then follow the signs to the riverside.

Explore on foot

The historic centre is compact and best explored slowly. Allow about an hour for the old town loop, or two hours with a riverside detour. Some older lanes have uneven cobbles.

Make it a family day

Choose the gentle riverside walk, leave time for the meadow, and stop at the market for something delicious. Check individual event details for suggested ages.

Rain in the forecast?

Start with the heritage rooms, spend a little longer in the bookshop, and settle into a café. A slower afternoon can be the best kind.
